1. PROFESSIONAL FULL DEPTH DOM / PRICE LADDER

Replace the current separated long ASK and BID presentation in the dedicated DOM view with a continuous professional price ladder optimized for iPhone.

The visual concept should resemble a professional trading DOM: extremely easy to read, compact rows, price and liquidity visible immediately, but keep the existing LEX visual identity.

Support:

* 20 / 50 / 100 / 200 levels per side where the data source supports it.
* Default = 100 levels per side.
* Target Full Depth mode = up to 200 ASK + 200 BID.
* If Gate.io provides fewer levels through the current feed, display only real received levels. Never fabricate depth.

The ladder should conceptually look like:

ASK 313.66 | size | cumulative | age/status
ASK 313.65 | size | cumulative | age/status
ASK 313.64 | 4.82 | cumulative | WALL 01:24
ASK 313.63 | size | cumulative

–––– LIVE PRICE 313.62 ––––

BID 313.61 | size | cumulative
BID 313.60 | size | cumulative
BID 313.59 | 7.83 | cumulative | WALL 00:48
BID 313.58 | size | cumulative

The market boundary must move naturally as best bid/ask and trades change.

* Incoming WebSocket updates must NEVER move the user’s viewport.

FOLLOW PRICE remains OFF by default.

When FOLLOW PRICE = ON, keep the live market area approximately centered.
